Property Overview

Morrissey Manor stands as one of the most iconic residence halls on the University of Notre Dame campus. Originally completed in 1925, this historic Gothic structure has been thoughtfully modernized through two major renovation phases in 1997–1998 and 2018–2019, preserving its architectural heritage while upgrading student accommodations and building systems to contemporary standards.

The property encompasses approximately 65,000 square feet and houses 95 residential units across a diverse mix of singles, doubles, triples, quads, and four premium suite apartments featuring private living areas and bathrooms. The Manor's amenity package includes multiple lounge spaces, dedicated study areas, full kitchens, a fitness facility, private chapel, and "Yaz's," the widely acclaimed student-run restaurant that serves as a unique revenue-generating amenity.

With its distinguished façade, expansive landscaped front lawn, and generous outdoor patio, Morrissey Manor combines architectural distinction, resident comfort, and prime campus positioning into a compelling institutional-grade investment opportunity.

Location & Market Position

Morrissey Manor occupies a premier position on the western perimeter of Notre Dame's historic South Quad, commonly referred to as the "Gold Coast" for its collection of distinguished residence halls. The property benefits from exceptional walkability to major campus destinations including the Rockne Memorial athletic center, South Dining Hall, LaFortune Student Center, the Basilica of the Sacred Heart, and the iconic Main Building.

Residents enjoy immediate access to academic facilities, dining options, and Notre Dame Stadium. The broader South Bend market provides additional amenities with downtown South Bend, regional retail centers, and South Bend International Airport all within convenient driving distance. This central, highly connected location ensures consistent demand from the university's stable student population.
